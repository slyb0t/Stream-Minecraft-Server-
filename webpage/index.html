Server Features Guide

Welcome! We use a few lightweight plugins to enhance survival gameplay, protect your builds, and keep the community connected.

🛡️Land Claiming

Don't let griefers ruin your hard work! We use SimpleClaimSystem to let you protect your land, chests, and builds.

  • Protect your base from other players breaking blocks.
  • Prevent others from opening your chests or doors.
  • Manage who you trust on your land.
Tip: Type /claim help or check the starting inventory books to learn how to start claiming land!

☠️Death Chests

Dying is stressful, but losing your items shouldn't be. We use DeathChest to safely store your inventory when you fall.

  • When you die, a chest spawns at your location containing your items.
  • Your items won't despawn or explode everywhere.
  • Hurry back to collect your gear!
Coordinates to your death chest usually appear in chat when you die.

💬Discord Chat Link

Stay connected even when you aren't on the server. DiscordSRV bridges Minecraft chat with our Discord server.

  • Talk to players in-game from the Discord channel.
  • See Discord messages while playing Minecraft.
  • See who joins and leaves in real-time.
Don't forget to join our Discord server to use this feature!

⚙️Server Essentials

These plugins run in the background to keep things running smoothly and looking nice.

  • LuckPerms: Handles player ranks, prefixes in chat, and ensures you have the right permissions to use commands.
  • MiniMOTD: Provides the neat descriptions and icons you see in your multiplayer server list before joining.
Running on Paper Minecraft 1.21.11
